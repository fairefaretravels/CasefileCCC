Where to send disputes

These are the three national credit bureaus' current dispute-mail addresses and phone lines. Certified mail with return receipt is worth the extra few dollars — it's your proof of the date they received it, which is what starts their legal response clock.
Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act, a bureau generally must investigate a dispute within 30 days of receiving it (occasionally 45). If a specific creditor or collector reported the error, you can also dispute directly with them — add them as a custom contact in the Draft Letter tab.
